1. About PodcastersFriend

PodcastersFriend coordinates paid podcast-to-podcast promotional campaigns. Buyers purchase promotional reach, and participating podcast hosts may accept paid opportunities to introduce or recommend other podcasts to their audiences.

2. Campaigns and reach

Campaign reach is planned using recent audience or episode-download information associated with participating podcasts. Download-based reach is a measurement basis for campaign inventory and does not mean every download represents a unique person who heard or acted on a promotion.

If qualifying placements collectively fall short of the purchased estimated reach during the applicable measurement window, PodcastersFriend may provide eligible additional or replacement placements as a make-good for the shortfall.

3. No guarantee of results

PodcastersFriend does not guarantee new followers, subscribers, listeners, downloads, sales, leads, revenue, rankings, chart position, or other downstream results. Results depend on factors outside our control, including audience fit, the promoted show's concept and catalog, listener behavior, platform behavior, and host delivery.

4. Matching and placement

Unless otherwise expressly agreed, buyers purchase a managed campaign rather than placement on a specific named podcast. PodcastersFriend determines the campaign mix based on audience fit, available inventory, campaign requirements, and other relevant factors. The number of participating shows may vary.

Podcast publishing schedules vary. An expected campaign window may be provided before payment or confirmation, but individual placement dates can change.

Buyers must provide accurate information about their podcast and may be asked for show links, descriptions, audience information, positioning, and key talking points. Buyers represent that they have the right to provide and authorize use of submitted materials and that their claims are truthful and lawful.

6. Promotion Network participation

Joining the Promotion Network is free and does not guarantee paid opportunities. Hosts may accept or decline each opportunity. Eligibility may depend on audience fit, recent download performance, availability, content suitability, verification, and campaign requirements.

Standard opportunities may use a $25 CPM host payout with a $50 minimum placement payout unless a different amount is stated for a specific opportunity. PodcastersFriend may request reasonable audience or analytics verification.

7. Host responsibilities and disclosure

Hosts accepting a placement agree to provide the agreed promotion accurately and within the agreed parameters. Hosts are responsible for complying with applicable laws, platform rules, and disclosure requirements for paid endorsements or sponsored promotional content.

8. Acceptance and suitability

PodcastersFriend may decline, pause, replace, or cancel a proposed match or campaign when reasonably necessary for audience suitability, safety, legality, platform compliance, suspected fraud, inaccurate information, unavailable inventory, or other legitimate business reasons. Hosts retain the right to decline individual opportunities.

9. Payments, cancellations, and refunds

Campaign pricing and payment terms are presented before purchase. Because podcast inventory may be reserved or committed, cancellation and refund eligibility may depend on campaign status. Campaign-specific terms provided at purchase will control. If PodcastersFriend cannot reasonably fulfill a paid campaign and cannot provide a suitable replacement or make-good, an appropriate full or partial refund may be issued for the unfulfilled portion.

10. Fraud and manipulation

Artificially inflated downloads, bot traffic, falsified analytics, fraudulent audience information, or other manipulation are prohibited. PodcastersFriend may remove participants, withhold payment associated with fraudulent or unverifiable activity where legally permitted, or take other reasonable protective action.
